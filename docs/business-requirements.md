# Apex Distribution & Logistics
## Business Requirements

### Company
Apex Distribution & Logistics is a distribution company that purchases
inventory from suppliers, stores it in warehouses, and sells it to customers.

### Initial ERP Scope

The initial implementation will cover:

- Purchasing
- Inventory
- Sales
- Accounts Payable
- Accounts Receivable
- General Ledger
- Basic Cost/Profitability analysis

### First Business Process

Procure-to-Pay (P2P)

Process:

Purchase Requirement
→ Purchase Order
→ Goods Receipt
→ Supplier Invoice
→ Supplier Payment

### Initial Business Requirement

Apex must be able to purchase inventory from approved suppliers,
receive the inventory into a warehouse, record supplier invoices,
and record payments to suppliers.

### ERP Objectives

The ERP system should provide:

1. Centralized master data
2. Inventory visibility
3. Purchase transaction tracking
4. Supplier payable tracking
5. Financial transaction recording
6. Basic reporting

### SAP S/4HANA Reference

Relevant SAP areas:

- MM — Purchasing and Inventory Management
- FI-AP — Accounts Payable
- FI-GL — General Ledger
- CO — Controlling

ERPNext is being used as the practical implementation platform.
Its functionality and architecture should not be assumed to be identical
to SAP S/4HANA.
